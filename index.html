Optical Tactile Sensing for Underwater Robotics

paper github

Researcher · GRASP Lab, University of Pennsylvania · with Annie Yang & Prof. Cynthia Sung · 2025

Teaching a soft underwater robot to feel using only a camera. We print a dense fractal maze pattern on a transparent elastomer skin (225 × 30 mm), mount a fisheye camera inside, and train a spatiotemporal encoder-decoder to infer contact location, indentation depth, normal force, and surface wave propagation at 30 fps — no force sensors at inference.

  • Custom data rig collected ~360,000 labeled frames across 1,076 grid locations on a 2 mm × 2 mm grid
  • Analytically derived labels via spatially-varying Hertzian contact mechanics + elastic surface tension; per-strip (E, γ) calibration
  • ResNet18 backbone + dense optical flow + ConvLSTM over 16-frame window; masked heads for static vs. wave estimation
  • Baseline MAE: 0.52 mm contact location · 0.38 mm depth · 55 mN force — generalizes to unseen contact positions

NeuroMotus: Brain-Computer Interface for Cerebral Palsy

paper poster video

Independent Research · Oregon Health & Science University · 2022 – 2024

Intent-based movement prediction using EEG signals to expand exoskeleton capabilities for Cerebral Palsy patients beyond basic walking. Decodes Bereitschaftspotential signals with a custom CNN and drives a ROS-controlled limb simulator via a bidirectional neurofeedback loop.

  • 94% accuracy detecting movement intent from raw EEG; 87% accuracy across 6 essential movement types
  • 23.3% faster response time vs. prior systems; OpenBCI Cyton board with custom dry electrode cap
  • Bidirectional loop: EEG → CNN → ROS virtual limb → visual feedback → EEG
  • 2nd Place Grand Award & CIA Award, ISEF 2023

Robotic Door Manipulation via Deep Reinforcement Learning

video

Course Project · University of Pennsylvania · 2024

DDPG actor-critic reinforcement learning system for robotic door manipulation in the RoboSuite simulation environment. Achieved near-perfect task success through reward shaping and exploration noise scheduling.

  • 98% task success rate on door-opening from a continuous action space
  • Mean episodic return improved from 90 → 230 over 7,000 training steps
  • DDPG with target network soft updates; Ornstein-Uhlenbeck exploration noise

MusTENG: Wearable Triboelectric Nanogenerator for Cerebral Palsy Rehabilitation

paper journal

Independent Research · Published in Journal of Research: High School, Vol. 2022(2) pp. 168–180 · 2022

Wearable triboelectric nanogenerator sensor and ML pipeline for quantifying muscle spasticity in children with Cerebral Palsy. Converts microscopic muscle deformation into electrical signals; a 1D convolutional autoencoder computes a Calculated Muscle Spasticity Score (cMSS) across three leg muscle groups.

  • Custom woven sensor (65% polyester / 100% nylon + silver conductor) fabricated and calibrated from scratch
  • 1D convolutional autoencoder (7 layers, 9,500+ parameters) with AUC-based anomaly scoring
  • 91% accuracy on thigh · 84% hamstring · 89% calf across 170 trials; three CP gait patterns validated
  • Closed-loop physical therapy game using live cMSS sensor feedback
  • Peer-reviewed publication, Journal of Research: High School (2022)
