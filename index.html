HA-SLAM / Camera trajectory estimation

Human Archive · Technical report · July 2026

HA-SLAM: Egocentric Visual–Inertial SLAM

Ishan Ahluwalia, Samay Maini

technical report

Stereo-inertial SLAM for head-worn cameras under motion blur, low light, and rapid head rotation.

  • Learned feature extraction and matching, egocentric backend optimization, and cross-modal metric self-calibration.
  • Reported mean raw trajectory error of 0.54 cm at 99.0% tracking completeness on Human Archive’s motion-capture benchmark.
02
HARD2 / Dexterous manipulation

Human Archive · Research project · October 2026

HARD2: Human-Video Pretraining for Dexterous Manipulation

project page

A controlled study of how egocentric human data transfers to robot manipulation. I worked on this project at Human Archive.

  • Compared three human-video pretraining datasets and a no-pretraining baseline using VITRA and matched robot demonstrations.
  • Evaluated on a Franka FR3 with a Wuji Hand 2: 200 trials across four tasks.
  • Human Archive pretraining achieved 40% task success on new objects, versus 28% and 24% for the other datasets and 12% without human-video pretraining.

03
Optical tactile sensing / Elastomer skin

GRASP Lab, University of Pennsylvania · Research project

Optical Tactile Sensing for Underwater Robotics

With Annie Yang and Prof. Cynthia Sung

manuscript / code

Camera-based estimation of contact location, indentation depth, and force from the deformation of a patterned elastomer skin.

  • Built a pipeline connecting controlled indentation, synchronized camera and load-cell recordings, pattern extraction, and learned estimation.
  • The documented ResNet18 baseline reports held-out-location MAE of 0.52 mm in location Y, 0.38 mm in indentation, and 55 mN in force.
